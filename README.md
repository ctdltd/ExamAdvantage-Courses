### 26324.01 - 2026-11-20

CMMC Certified Assessor (CCA) and CMMC Certified Professional (CCP)

Two new ISACA courses for the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification credentials. ISACA has issued these credentials as the CMMC Assessor and Instructor Certification Organization (CAICO) since December 2025. Phase 2 of the CMMC rollout began on November 10, 2026: contracting officers can now require a Level 2 certification, assessed by a C3PAO, as a condition of award.

CMMC Certified Professional (CCP)	10,350	24,168	10,350	84.2 MB
CMMC Certified Assessor (CCA)	6,767	15,477	6,767	55.1 MB

Needs program 26324 or later. Earlier versions do not list these two courses. Update the program first (desktop: Update now on the banner; Android: Google Play), then download the courses under ISACA in the course list.

CMMC Certified Professional (CCP)

The entry credential of the CMMC ecosystem and the prerequisite for the CCA. The course follows the six domains of the exam outline:

Domain	Pages	Questions
1. CMMC Ecosystem	510	1,230
2. CMMC-AB Code of Professional Conduct (Ethics)	419	1,053
3. CMMC Governance and Source Documents	1,447	3,570
4. CMMC Model Construct and Implementation Evaluation	6,701	15,273
5. CMMC Assessment Process (CAP)	848	2,046
6. Scoping	425	996

Written for a team member who works under a Lead CCA: gathering and reviewing evidence, supporting determinations and staying within the role.

CMMC Certified Assessor (CCA)

For assessors who conduct Level 2 certification assessments on a C3PAO team and make determinations. The course follows the four domains of the exam outline:

Domain	Pages	Questions
1. Evaluating Organizations Seeking Certification (OSC) Against CMMC Level 2	289	666
2. CMMC Level 2 Assessment Scoping	700	1,689
3. CMMC Assessment Process (CAP)	500	1,200
4. Assessing CMMC Level 2 Practices	5,278	11,922

Domain 4 covers all 110 Level 2 practices, from AC.L2-3.1.1 to SI.L2-3.14.7, down to their assessment objectives and methods.

What every page holds
Two overview paragraphs, a Deep Dive and a Real-World Example with a named practitioner
A figure: a flow, model or map of the topic
A Relevance callout tying the topic to the exam objective and domain, and an Exam Tip
On every topic page, three scenario questions (easy, moderate and challenging), each with an explanation of the correct answer and of every wrong one

The questions put you in the assessor’s seat: scoping CUI assets, Security Protection Assets, Contractor Risk Managed Assets and specialized assets; judging whether evidence is adequate and sufficient; scoring with the DoD Assessment Methodology; POA&M eligibility and closeout; and what a CCP may and may not decide.

Current as of November 2026
32 CFR Part 170 (effective December 16, 2024) and the DFARS 252.204-7021 phase-in (Phase 1 November 10, 2025; Phase 2 November 10, 2026)
NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 for Level 2 (the revision CMMC uses), SP 800-171A objectives, SP 800-172 for Level 3
The CMMC Level 2 Assessment and Scoping Guides, version 2.13
Scoring from 110 down to -203, Conditional status at 88 points, POA&M closeout within 180 days
The CMMC Status Date as 32 CFR 170.4 defines it: the date results are submitted to SPRS or CMMC eMASS, unchanged after a POA&M closeout
The Cyber AB (formerly the CMMC-AB) as the Accreditation Body, ISACA as the CAICO
FIPS 140-3 for new cryptographic modules (FIPS 140-2 certificates moved to the CMVP Historical List on September 22, 2026), NIST SP 800-63-4 for digital identity
How the courses were checked
Every practice question was read for two right answers, a wrong key, an absurd distractor or a broken stem; 3,218 were repaired.
Answer length gives nothing away: the correct option is the longest, second, third or shortest about equally often.
Fact check: 119,658 sentences and answer fields that cite a rule, a number, a deadline or a CMMC body were checked against current sources, with a second review of every proposed correction. 1,207 corrections were applied; pages that described the wrong NIST requirement for a practice were then rewritten as a whole.
Glossary: 44 CMMC terms added (credentials, the Cyber AB and the CAICO, eMASS, the asset categories, CMMC Status Date, Conditional and Final status, enduring exception, temporary deficiency, FedRAMP Moderate equivalency and others).
